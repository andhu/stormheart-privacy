Stormheart Privacy Policy

Game: Stormheart (Android) · Developer: Detoner · Last updated: 23 November 2026

Stormheart is made by one independent developer. This page explains, in plain words, what information the game sends off your phone, why, and what you can do about it. We don't sell your data and don't use it for advertising.

What the game sends

Crash and error reports

If the game crashes, freezes or hits an error, it sends a report so we can fix it: the error details, your phone model, Android version, the game version and a random player ID made by the game. It does not include your name, contacts, location or advertising ID. Processed by Sentry.

Bug reports you choose to send

Test versions of the game have a report button. Nothing is sent unless you press it. A report contains what you write, a screenshot of the game, any pictures you choose to add, an optional name you type, your phone model, Android version, screen size, the game version, your game progress (stage, hero level, Power) and the latest game log. Processed by Supabase.

Game logs (test versions)

Test versions send gameplay statistics so we can balance the game: stages played, results, damage numbers, choices made, frame rate, and a short device note (phone model, Android version, graphics chip, memory, language, screen size) with a random install ID. Processed by Supabase.

Storm Hall leaderboards

To place you on the leaderboards the game sends a random player ID, the player name you pick (or one the game makes up), your scores and Power, your equipped gear and skills, the week you started and the game version. Other players can see your player name, scores and equipped loadout. Please don't use your real name or personal details as a player name. Processed by Supabase.

What we don't collect

  • No advertising ID, no location, no contacts, no photos except the ones you add to a bug report yourself.
  • No payment information (the game has no purchases at the moment).
  • No accounts with email or password: the game signs in anonymously with its random player ID.

Why we use it

Only to run the game's online features (leaderboards), fix bugs and crashes, and balance the game. We don't sell or share it with anyone for advertising or marketing.

Who processes it

Two service providers store and process this data for us on their servers: Sentry (crash reports) and Supabase (bug reports, game logs, leaderboards). They may only use it to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

Crash reports are deleted automatically by Sentry after its retention period (about 90 days). Bug reports, game logs and leaderboard entries are kept while we develop and run the game, and we delete them sooner if you ask.

Your choices

  • Bug reports are optional: just don't press the report button.
  • To delete your data, email us with your player name and its tag (for example Stormrider#1234, shown on the name screen). We'll delete your leaderboard entry, reports and logs.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on your phone.

Children

Stormheart is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If the game starts collecting anything new (for example, when purchases or ads are added), we'll update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
